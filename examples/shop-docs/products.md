# Product care and warranty

## Sizing

Each product page has a size chart. If you're between two sizes, we suggest the larger one for jackets and the smaller one for base layers. You can exchange for another size within 30 days of delivery.

## Caring for waterproof jackets

Wash waterproof jackets on a cold, gentle cycle with a technical wash, not regular detergent or fabric softener. Tumble dry on low to restore the water-repellent finish.

## Warranty

Larkspur-brand products have a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the delivery date. It doesn't cover normal wear, accidents or misuse. Products from other brands follow their maker's warranty. To make a claim, contact our team with your order number and photos of the defect.
