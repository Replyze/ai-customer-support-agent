# Returns and refunds

## Return window

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery. Items marked Final Sale can't be returned.

## How to start a return

1. Go to https://larkspur.example/returns and enter your order number and email address.
2. Choose the items you're sending back and tell us why.
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the parcel at any UPS location.

A return label costs $6.95, which we take off your refund. Exchanges and returns of faulty items get a free label.

## Exchanges

To exchange an item for another size or color, start a return and choose Exchange. We ship the new item as soon as UPS scans your return.

## Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days after your return arrives at our warehouse. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. Original shipping costs are not refunded.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact our team within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We send a replacement or a refund, and you don't pay for the return.
