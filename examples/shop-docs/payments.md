# Payments

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Larkspur gift cards. We don't accept cash on delivery, checks or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If only part of the order ships, you are charged only for that part.

## Currency and sales tax

All amounts on the site are in US dollars. Canadian orders are also charged in US dollars, and your bank converts the amount. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

## Promo codes

You can use one promo code per order. Enter it at checkout before you pay. A code can't be added to an order that has already been placed.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts from $25 to $200 and never expire. You can check a gift card's balance at https://larkspur.example/gift-cards.
