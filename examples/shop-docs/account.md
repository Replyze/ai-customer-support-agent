# Your account

## Create an account

You don't need an account to order. With one, you can see your orders, save addresses and start returns faster. Create one at https://larkspur.example/account.

## Reset your password

Click Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send a reset link that works for 1 hour. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Change your email address

Sign in, go to Account settings and choose Change email. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it.

## Close your account

Ask our team to close your account. We remove your personal details within 30 days, apart from order records we must keep for tax purposes.
