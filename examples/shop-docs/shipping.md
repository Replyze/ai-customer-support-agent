# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii, and to Canada. We don't ship to other countries yet, and we can't deliver to P.O. boxes.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 1 pm Pacific Time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and US holidays, leave on the next business day.

## Delivery times

- Standard shipping in the US takes 3 to 5 business days after your order leaves the warehouse.
- Express shipping in the US takes 1 to 2 business days after your order leaves the warehouse.
- Shipping to Canada takes 5 to 10 business days. Customs can add a few days.

## Shipping costs

- Standard shipping in the US costs $5.95, and it is free on orders of $75 or more.
- Express shipping in the US costs $14.95.
- Shipping to Canada costs $12.95 for every order. Duties and taxes are paid on delivery.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also enter your order number and email address on the Track order page: https://larkspur.example/track. A new tracking number can take up to 24 hours to show its first update.
