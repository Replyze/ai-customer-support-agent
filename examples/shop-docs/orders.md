# Orders

## Cancel or change an order

You can cancel or change an order within 1 hour of placing it, from the link in your order confirmation email. After that, the order is already being packed and can't be changed, but you can return it once it arrives.

## Change the shipping address

If your order hasn't shipped yet, our team can change the address. Write to us with your order number and the new address. Once an order has shipped, the address can't be changed.

## Order confirmation email

We send a confirmation email within a few minutes of your order. If you can't find it, check your spam or promotions folder. If you have an account, every order is also listed under Orders.

## Out-of-stock items

If an item in your order sells out before we pack it, we email you and refund that item. The rest of your order ships as usual.
